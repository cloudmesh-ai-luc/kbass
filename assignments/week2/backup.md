# Assignment W2.3: Backup Your Computer

## Why Backing Up a Computer Is Important

Backing up a computer is important because it protects important files from accidental deletion, hardware failure, malware, theft, or other unexpected events. A backup provides another copy of important data that can be restored if the original files are lost or damaged. Without a backup, recovering personal documents, school assignments, photos, and other important files can be difficult or impossible. Regular backups also reduce the risk of losing recent work after a system failure. Maintaining a consistent backup routine is therefore an important part of protecting data and maintaining access to important information.

## Three Real-World Consequences of Not Having a Backup

1. **Permanent data loss:** A hard drive failure can cause important documents, photos, and other files to be permanently lost.
2. **Loss of school or work:** Important assignments, projects, reports, or work-related files may have to be recreated from the beginning.
3. **Financial and operational impact:** Businesses can lose customer information, records, and other critical data, potentially resulting in financial losses and extended downtime.

## Backup Method: External Hard Drive

An external hard drive can be used to create a local copy of important files. The drive should have enough storage capacity for the files being backed up and should be disconnected when the backup is complete to reduce the risk of accidental deletion or malware affecting the backup.

### Setup Steps

1. Connect the external hard drive to the computer using a USB connection.
2. Confirm that the computer recognizes the external drive and that sufficient storage space is available.
3. Create a clearly labeled backup folder on the external drive.
4. Select important files and folders to back up, such as documents, school assignments, photos, and other personal data.
5. Copy the selected files to the backup folder on the external hard drive.
6. Verify that the copied files are present and can be opened from the backup drive.
7. Safely eject the external hard drive after the backup is complete.
8. Store the external hard drive in a safe location and repeat the backup on a regular schedule.

## Weekly Backup Schedule

| Day | Time | What to Back Up |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | 7:00 PM | Documents, school assignments, projects, photos, and important personal files |

The weekly backup will be performed every Sunday at 7:00 PM. The backup will include newly created or modified files from the previous week. After verification, the external hard drive will be safely disconnected and stored in a secure location.

## Cloud Computing Incident Case

A well-known example of the importance of backups is the **2014 Code Spaces incident**. Code Spaces was a cloud-based code hosting and collaboration company that experienced a major security incident after an attacker gained access to its Amazon Web Services management console. The attacker attempted to delete the company's data and backups. Because the attacker was able to access and delete critical resources, Code Spaces was unable to recover its operations and ultimately shut down. The incident demonstrates why backups should be protected separately from the primary environment and why backup access should have strong security controls.

## Conclusion

A reliable backup strategy helps protect important data from hardware failure, accidental deletion, security incidents, and other unexpected events. Using an external hard drive provides a simple way to maintain a separate copy of important files. Performing backups on a consistent weekly schedule and verifying the backed-up files can significantly reduce the risk of permanent data loss.
